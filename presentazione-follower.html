Welcome to the most exclusive digital ecosystem where glamour, fashion culture, and luxury entertainment merge into direct, authentic, and unedited interaction.

1. Discovery Wall & Exclusive Creator Slots

Inside our private ecosystem, you gain access to the Discovery Wall, the elite showcase populated by the most sought-after creators and stars of the moment. To preserve the club's absolute value, global access starts at an initial perimeter capped at just 5,000 active creator slots.

Expansion Mechanism: Once the initial 5,000 slots are filled, the platform progressively unlocks additional blocks of 1,000 creators at a time (expanding the limit to 6,000, 7,000 stars, and beyond). This guarantees constant scarcity while allowing the platform to continually welcome new flows of creators and fresh talent for you to discover.

2. Your Personal Bacheca (Up to 50 Favorite Stars)

Within your personal dashboard, you can use custom checkmarks to select and pin up to a maximum of 50 favorite stars of your absolute interest onto your bacheca.

This keeps your preferred creators organized and under control, allowing you to monitor their activity, evaluate their profiles at your own pace, and decide the exact moment to subscribe and engage.

3. Monthly Subscriptions & Messaging Privileges

To unlock your favorite creators' universes and chat directly with them, you can activate a monthly subscription. Messaging and interaction privileges scale based on your chosen tier:

  • Starter ($50 / month): Your entry pass to unlock the private feed and follow daily core content.
  • Advance ($150 / month): Access to high-privacy multimedia sets with a dedicated messaging channel and priority delivery.
  • Elite ($300 / month): Exclusive early previews and high-priority chat channels to communicate directly with the star.
  • VIP ($500 / month): The pinnacle of the NGS experience: top-tier status, absolute response priority in messaging, and exclusive reserved perks.

5. Exclusive Galleries, Locked Media & Pay-Per-View (PPV)

Creators constantly publish exclusive multimedia content. Across the feeds, you will encounter private photos and videos that are completely blurred and locked:

  • If you hold a higher-tier subscription (e.g., Elite or VIP): Many of these exclusive contents are automatically unlocked or included directly in your package.
  • If you hold a base or lower tier: Blurred private photos and videos require a choice. The creator decides the price to unlock that specific content. You can either wait for the creator to unlock it for free over time, or pay the exact price set by the creator to instantly reveal and view the private photo or video.

Your peace of mind is protected by top-tier security standards. All transactions, subscriptions, and PPV unlocks are handled securely and discreetly through Stripe's global infrastructure, ensuring banking-grade encryption and complete shielding of your financial details on your bank statements (zero explicit or compromising descriptions).
